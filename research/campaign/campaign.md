# Campaign Rush: PoE 2 (0.5.x, ahead of 1.0)

Researched 2026-11-21. Covers the current campaign (0.5 / 0.5.5: Acts 1-4, then Interludes 1-3; there has been no Cruel since 0.3, Aug 2025 [C1]).
1.0 launches 2026-12-11 with Acts 5-6, and **the Interludes are being removed** [C17]. See section 8.

**How to read the tags:**
- **[S]** sourced, with the source ID from section 9;
- **[E]** my estimate, or general game knowledge not checked against a 0.5 source;
- confidence is H, M or L.

The machine-readable version is `campaign.json`, next to this file.

---

## 1. The short version

1. **Rush, but never skip a permanent reward.** Points, spirit, resistances and life come from about 30 stops (section 5). Everything else is optional. [S C1, C2, C4] H
2. **Kill blue (magic) packs, skip white packs and most rares.** Magic packs are said to give about 3.5x the XP of white packs. [S C4, C6] M
3. **Movement speed is the biggest speed upgrade:** 10% boots from level 1, 15% by about level 16, 20% at 33, 25% at 46. [S C2] H
4. **Upgrade flasks at levels 4, 10, 16, 23, 30, 40, 50 and 60.** [S C2, C4] H
5. **Resistances gate the bosses.** Have 30-40% cold for Count Geonor (end of Act 1), fire for Act 2's mines and Act 4, lightning for Jamanra and Doryani. [S C2, C4] H
6. **First ascendancy:** do the Sekhemas trial at about level 22 with Balbala's Barya. Racers wait until about 28 and use a Djinn Barya from the Watchful Twins in Deshar, so they can skip Balbala. [S C4, C6] M
7. **Second ascendancy:** the Trial of Chaos in Act 3, right after Xyclucian (about level 38-40). [S C2, C3] H
8. **Use Escape to skip NPC dialogue (new in 0.5).** Vendor once per act, not after every upgrade. [S C8, C4] H

## 3. Act by act

**0.5 layout changes [S C8] H:**
- The Dreadnought Vanguard is gone; you fight Jamanra in the Dreadnought.
- Act 3's areas are in a new order.
- The Waterways levers are now pressure pads, and the canal starts drained.
- Late-campaign and Interlude monster density is lower.
- Areas have hints showing where to go.

**Old routes are unreliable.** A GGG designer said that so much changed in 0.5 that memorised layouts would be useless [S C16 snippet] M.

### Act 1 (levels 1 to about 14-15)
- **Do:**
  - Beira in Clearfell (+10% cold);
  - Crowbell in the Hunting Grounds (+2 points; go there first when the paths split [C4]);
  - the King in the Mists in Freythorn (Ritual altars, +30 spirit);
  - Una's Lute in the Ogham Farmlands (+2);
  - Candlemass in Ogham Manor (+20 life);
  - the Salvage Bench in Ogham Village. [S C1, C2] H
- **Also take:**
  - the free Medium flask from the Grelwood witch-hut cauldron;
  - the Sapphire Charm reward for the Geonor fight;
  - the Executioner's Perfect Strike gem, if you play Warrior. [S C2, C7] M
- **Skip:**
  - Brambleghast (unless you want its uncut gem);
  - the Mud Burrow Devourer;
  - optional caches. [S C2] M
- **Gates:** buy a Greater life flask by level 10, and have 30-40% cold resistance before Count Geonor. [S C2, C4] H

### Act 2 (about 15-30)
- **Do:**
  - Keth first when the desert map opens (Kabala, +2);
  - the Valley of the Titans relic altar (charm choice, can be swapped later);
  - The Final Letter in Deshar (+2);
  - the Sisters of Garukhan shrine in the Spires of Deshar (+10% lightning);
  - the first ascendancy (section 4). [S C1, C2, C4] H
- **Racer trick:** skip Balbala in the Traitor's Passage, farm the Watchful Twins' Djinn Barya in Deshar, and run the trial at about level 28 [S C4, C6] M.
- **Skip:**
  - Lost City optional bosses;
  - full clears of the Mastodon Badlands. The Bone Pits are still needed for the trunks quest [E] M.
- **Gates:**
  - fire resistance before the Mawdun mines;
  - lightning (plus some fire) for Jamanra.
  - The Dreadnought has a checkpoint full of blue packs, good for a level or two if you're behind. [S C2, C4] M
- **Vendors:**
  - Zarka (end of the act): Colossal flasks and an uncut support gem;
  - the Spires of Deshar: level-30 items, 20% movement-speed boots, Solar Amulet. [S C2] M

### Act 3 (about 30-42 in 0.5 routes; older guides say up to 45)
- **Do:**
  - Mighty Silverfist in the Jungle Ruins (+2);
  - the Venom Crypts draught (pick carefully: **it can't be changed later**);
  - Blackjaw in Jiquani's Machinarium (+10% fire);
  - Ignagduk in the Azak Bog (+30 spirit; needs the Matlan Waterways waypoint);
  - the Sacrificial Heart altar in Aggorat (+2);
  - Xyclucian in the Chimeral Wetlands, which gives the Trial of Chaos key. [S C1, C2] H
- **Molten Vault (Mektul, which unlocks the Reforging Bench):** a timed DPS check. If your damage is low, come back after the act [S C2] H.
- **Gates:**
  - Viper Napuatzi: chaos and fire resistance (an anti-poison charm helps);
  - Doryani: lightning. [S C4] M
- **Minion builds:** do the King in the Mists and Ignagduk early for the spirit [S C4] M.
- **In 0.5, the Fate of the Vaal is part of the base game:** six Ancient Beacons in Act 3 open the Vaal Ruins portal. They are optional for a pure rush [S C6, C8] M.

### Act 4 (about 42-52)
- **The islands can be done in any order.** Leave Ngakanu and Arastas (Tavakai) for last [S C2] H.
- **Do:**
  - the Dark Mists quest (Tujen) → Omniphobia in Journey's End (+2, the Mist-shrouded Tome);
  - the Trial of the Ancestors (Hinekora, +2);
  - Silent Hall in the Eye of Hinekora (+5% mana);
  - the three Halls of the Dead tattoos (attribute or resistance, **locked once chosen**);
  - the Goddess of Justice in the Abandoned Prison (flask choice, swappable);
  - Whakapanu Island: the Singing Caverns, then the hideout quest. **This unlocks trading and the Currency Exchange.** [S C1, C2, C22] H
- **Optional:** the Great White One (shark fin) → Tribal Medicine choice; 30% defences is the strong pick [S C1, C24] M.
- **Skip:** optional treasure-map / Expedition islands [S C10] M.
- **Save** artificers, runes and whetstones for a weapon at the end of the act [S C6] M.
- **The Holten ferryman** sells Greater Runes once, for about 20k gold [S C2] M.

### Interludes 1-3 (about 52-60 in 0.5 routes; datapack area levels are 61-64)
- **In 0.5 they can be done in any order** [S C6] M.
- **Interlude 1:** Oswin in Wolvenhold (+2). [S C1] H
- **Interlude 2:**
  - go east first: Clearing the Way, the scorpion and worm bosses (+2; under 10 min) [S C4, C7];
  - Molten One's Gift in the Khari Crossing (+5% life);
  - the Seven Pillars in Qimah (pick one of seven, swappable). [S C1] H
- **Interlude 3:**
  - Lythara in Kriar Village (+40 spirit, the biggest single spirit source);
  - the Abominable Yeti in the Howling Caves (+2);
  - Elder Madox: a free unique (one pick). [S C1] H
- **Finish:**
  - the Hooded One in Kingsmarch ("Siege of Oriath") gives +2 once all Interludes are done. **Make sure your ascendancy choice is final first** [S C1, C6] M.
  - Maps open after that.

## 4. Ascendancy trials: when to do them

| Points | Trial | Key | Best timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 (picks the class) | Sekhemas (Act 2) | Balbala's Barya (Traitor's Passage), about level 22 | About 22-24. Racers wait to about 28 with the Watchful Twins' Barya | Relics help; Honour = life + ES. 0.5 adds a Reforging Bench at the entrance, and Runic Ward adds to starting Honour [S C3, C4, C6, C8] H |
| 3-4 | Chaos (Act 3) | Chimeral Inscribed Ultimatum (Xyclucian), about 38-40 | Straight after Xyclucian | Repeatable. Have rounded resistances (fire and cold first). 0.5.5: runs can be paused per room [S C2, C3, C9] H |
| 5-6 | Sekhemas, **level 60+** Djinn Barya (3 trials), OR Chaos, **level 65+** Ultimatum (10 rounds) | Random drop | Early maps, about 60-65 | [S C3] H |
| 7-8 | Sekhemas, **75+** Barya (4 trials + boss), OR Chaos, **65+** Ultimatum + pinnacle + all 3 Fates | Drop or trade | Endgame | [S C3] H |

- **Check against `poe2kit/route.py`:** TRIAL_LEVELS = [22, 38, 55, 70].
  - **The 3rd trial at 55 is too early.** Keys start at level 60 (Sekhemas) or 65 (Chaos), so 60-65 is a better default.
  - The 4th at 70 only works via Chaos (65+ with pinnacle and Fates); Sekhemas needs 75+.
- **Some 0.5.5 sources say either trial can come first** (Kami-labs: "Sekhemas or Chaos for your first Ascendancy") [S C9] M.
- A Chaos-first start suits builds that hate Honour, such as low-life or ES builds hit by Corrosive Concoction [S C3] M.

## 6. Gear, gems and the rush-vs-clear trade-off

**Movement speed [S C2] H:**
- 10% from level 1: transmute and augment every boot;
- 15% by about 16 (check vendors before the Mawdun Mines);
- 20% at 33 (Ziggurat vendors, the Infested Barrens chest);
- 25% at 46.

**Flasks [S C2] H:**
- Medium at 4 (free from the cauldron);
- Greater at 10 (Una);
- Grand at 16 (Act 1 vendors cap at item level 15, so buy it in Act 2);
- Giant at 23, Colossal at 30, Gargantuan at 40, Transcendent at 50, Ultimate at 60;
- the charge-gain suffix is worth having from level 8.

**Life (+ES) targets:** about 300 by the end of Act 1, 600 by Act 2, 900 by Act 3 [S C4] M.

**Gems:**
- Pour uncut skill gems into your main damage skill.
- Drop supports that raise mana cost for no gain.
- Gemcutter's Prisms are safe on a skill you'll keep [S C2] H.

**Weapons:**
- Rare or unique isn't better than a good magic weapon.
- Spend transmutes and augments freely, and only 1-2 regals per act.
- Expect to finish an act without a weapon upgrade, and don't stop to mule [S C2, C4, C6] M.

**XP:** stay within about 3 + level/16 of the area level [S C6] M. Lower density in 0.5 means fewer free levels from clearing [S C8] M.

**Rush vs full clear [E/S] M:**
- **Full clearing costs hours and pays little:**
  - campaign gear is replaced within an act;
  - XP from white packs is low;
  - the levels you'd gain catch up by maps anyway.
- **What a pure rush loses:**
  - **resistances.** You're 10% short per element per skipped buff, and Act 3-4 bosses punish that;
  - **spirit** (a 30-40 spirit gap can turn off a Herald or a minion);
  - **points.** Each skipped point quest is 2 points, and the Interlude ones have to be done anyway before the last +2.
- **Middle ground:** get every permanent reward and the league mechanic (each zone has a Ritual in 0.5.5) [S C9]. Skip white packs and optional bosses.
- **Market:** being early to the exchange pays most from about L3-12; see `research/league-start/league-start-deep.md`. So rush to the Whakapanu Island exchange unlock.

## 7. Common mistakes

1. **Full clearing every zone** out of fear of missing something. The plus-skull icon on the map (U panel) shows the permanent buffs [S C4; search snippets] M.
2. **Skipping permanent buff quests**, then hitting a resistance or spirit wall [S] M.
3. **Going into Count Geonor below 30% cold**, Napuatzi without chaos and fire resistance, or Jamanra and Doryani without lightning [S C2, C4] H.
4. **Old flasks and late flask use**; no charge-gain suffix [S C2] H.
5. **No movement-speed boots by level 16** [S C2, C7] H.
6. **Keeping rares or uniques over better magic items**, and spending Exalts in the campaign [S C2] H.
7. **Standing still or running in lines;** not hugging bosses [S C2] M.
8. **Vendoring after every upgrade**, and town trips mid-act [S C4, C6] M.
9. **Rushing the Molten Vault or other DPS checks without the damage.** Come back later instead [S C2] H.
10. **Picking the Venom Crypts draught or the tattoos without a plan:** they're locked [S C1, C2] H.
11. **Taking the final Interlude +2 turn-in before your ascendancy choice is settled** [S C6] L-M.
12. **No loot filter** [S C2] H.
13. **Following pre-0.5 layout routes** [S C8] M.
